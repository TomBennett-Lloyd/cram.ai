# Grounding standards

**Trigger:** writing a prompt, a retrieval query, a verifier call, or any code path that produces text the student reads.

## Rules

1. **Explanations and units only.** Model-produced text the student reads takes one of three shapes, each defined once in `@cram/shared` (`architecture.md` rule 2). An _explanation_ `{text, quotes[], sentences[], sources[]}` (`Explanation`) answers a question or writes a revision guide. A _unit_ `{text, citations[], kind}` (`Unit`), each citation `{sourceId, quote}`, is an exam question, gold answer, rubric point or lecture-summary line. A _lecture summary_ is a lecture's units. Model output reaches the student as one of those, or as one of exactly two things that carry no claim about the subject: a label from a closed enum (a verifier verdict, a grading verdict); and a topic tag (a noun phrase in the course index that the student can edit — metadata, never a statement). The list is closed and owned by `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (a).

2. **The pipeline order is fixed:** retrieve → generate → resolve → verify → report. No feature skips or reorders a stage. Generation begins by assembling the retrieved slides with their lectures' stored summaries under one prompt budget; nothing else enters the prompt as a source of facts. The explanation prompt asks for one explanation of what the sources say, in the model's own words, using its own knowledge to interpret the slides and never to add a fact about the course, with any quote a pointer to a numbered line; the unit prompt forbids claims the supplied sources do not support and requires at least one cited line per unit. At the light depth (rule 4) the verify stage is attribution and the contradiction mark. Owner: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (b), (j)–(l).

3. **Resolution precedes verification, and a quote is a substring or it is nothing.** The model never writes a quote: it cites a retrieved slide and the number of one of that slide's lines, and resolution looks the line up as the citation's `quote` (`resolveCitations`, #367). Every citation must name a source that exists, that was in the retrieved set for that call, _and_ whose text contains the citation's `quote` verbatim (whitespace-normalised, nothing else normalised). A citation failing any of the three makes its unit `dangling-citation`: withheld, never sent to the verifier. In an explanation the same checks apply to each quote pointer, against the slides assembled into the prompt rather than the whole retrieved set (`docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (c)), and one that fails is dropped as `dangling-citation` while its sentence stays whole. That covers a citation to a slide that exists but was not retrieved, and a line number that slide does not have. A unit with zero citations is `dangling-citation`. Resolution is mechanical; no model is involved, and after it nothing the model writes is string-matched (the verifier judges by meaning). Owner: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (c); the reason's name: `@cram/shared`'s `WithheldReason`.

4. **The verifier contracts, by depth and surface.** Each surface's depth is `VerificationDepth`, deep or light (`docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (f)). **Reported units (deep):** input is one unit's text plus the quoted spans of its citations — all of them, from however many sources — and nothing else; the verdict is judged against the union of the quotes; output is exactly one of `supported | partial | unsupported` (implementation: `docs/adr/0003-model-roles-and-backends.md` § Decision (d)). `unsupported` is withheld, always. `partial` is per feature and named: the feature's module declares one policy from the closed set — `drop`, or `rewrite-once` (the rewrite is re-verified like any unit; a second `partial` is dropped, and an `unsupported` rewrite is withheld as `unsupported`) — and the withheld report names the policy applied; a lecture-summary line is `drop`. No feature passes `partial` through. **Explanations at the deep depth (revision guides):** each sentence is scored against every assembled slide and summary line; a sentence any of them entails is never contradicted, a contradicted sentence that none entails is withheld, a neutral sentence is classified as a claim about the course (withheld as `unsupported`) or an interpretation (kept), and an explanation with no entailed sentence is withheld whole as `ungrounded`. **Explanations at the light depth (Ask):** nothing is withheld but a bad quote pointer; each sentence is attributed to the slides a search of the course finds for it, and one the cross-encoder scores as contradicting one of them, and as entailed by none, is marked and linked to that slide, never cut. Owner: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (d)–(g).

5. **"Not in your slides" is a result.** A request with no surviving unit, no shown explanation, or a model that finds nothing in the slides returns a fixed system string plus the closest retrieved slides. A prompt that overflows its budget drops summaries, then slides, and the student reads the left-out note, a fixed string beside it. It is a first-class result: never an error value, never a model-written apology, never a model-written answer from general knowledge. Owner: `CLAUDE.md` § Hard constraints, "Nothing the tutor says is uncited"; the left-out note: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (k).

6. **"Your slides say", never "this is true".** No prompt — generator, rewriter, verifier, topic tagger, grader — asserts the subject true or false, correct or incorrect, or compares it with general knowledge; a prompt describes what a cited slide says. No prompt instructs the model to add caveats, corrections or a "mainstream view". The explanation prompt asks the model to explain what the cited slides say, and the sentence-kind prompt asks what kind of sentence each is, never whether it is so. The contradiction mark reports what a slide says, never that a sentence is false. Owner: `CLAUDE.md` § Hard constraints, "The slides win".

7. **Withheld is reported, never silent.** Every withheld unit or sentence is surfaced to the student with its reason, one of `@cram/shared`'s `WithheldReason`, in a panel marked withheld; a contradicted sentence names its slide. That panel is the one place a failed unit's or sentence's text appears, and it is never rendered as an answer. At the light depth the panel holds dropped quote pointers only, each with its slide and line, and since a dropped pointer's sentence stays whole it is headed for them, never as withheld (#579). Owner: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (h); the reason vocabulary: `@cram/shared`'s `WithheldReason`.

8. **The contradiction set passes at 100% at the deep depth** — the bar `CLAUDE.md` § Hard constraints, "The slides win", sets. The set in `eval/` is the regression test for rule 6, and any deep-depth failure in it is red; the light depth's attribution recall, pass rate and contradiction-flag rate are reported beside it, never gated. How it gates a merge is `docs/adr/0005-evaluation-as-gate.md` § Decision (e)–(g)'s.

9. **Explanations synthesise; quotes point.** An explanation is written from all its assembled slides together, in the model's own words, and is never required to use slide wording; no prompt asks for extractive output, and no feature limits synthesis to ordering or headings. A quote is a marked pointer to one slide line, and unmarked text is the tutor's. What an explanation may not do is make a claim about the course its slides do not hold — rule 4 says how each depth checks it. A reported unit may not carry a claim its quotes do not entail. Every slide an explanation names is listed beneath it. Sources are slides today; a second source kind (the student's notes, #21) joins only by ADR. Owner: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (b), (c) and (i); `docs/adr/0001-grounding-architecture.md` § Decision (i), kept by it.

10. **Derived text is cited with its image, and exams draw only on confirmed derived text.** A source whose record is `derived: true` was read out of a picture. Ask and notes may cite it, and every citation, sources-list entry or reference to one is rendered beside the original slide image, labelled derived. Exam generation (questions, gold answers, rubric points) leaves every unconfirmed derived source out of its retrieved set, so rule 3 rejects a citation to one, and leaves every summary line citing one out of the summaries it assembles (#469). No prompt treats derived text differently from typed text. Owner: `docs/adr/0006-image-only-slides.md` § Decision (b), (f) and (g); the summaries the exclusion reaches: `docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md` § Decision (b).

## Why

The argument for retrieval and verification over fine-tuning or prompt-only grounding is recorded in [docs/adr/0001-grounding-architecture.md](../adr/0001-grounding-architecture.md), and the explanation answers and the two verification depths that supersede its contract in [docs/adr/0009-explanation-answers.md](../adr/0009-explanation-answers.md); these rules restate that decision as checks a feature can be held to. Every rule above names the section that owns it, so a change to the decision starts there.
